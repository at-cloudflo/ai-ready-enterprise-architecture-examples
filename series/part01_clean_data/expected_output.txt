-- One question, four answers: how much business is exposed to CMP-9? --
   what was summed                         total      why it differs
   --------------------------------------  ---------  ---------------------------
   every order line                        USD 7,400  includes fulfilled SO-106
   open orders only                        USD 6,200  includes valve order SO-107
   open orders containing a CMP-9 product  USD 4,550  counts SO-104's valve line
   open order lines that use CMP-9         USD 3,950  structural exposure
   Each step down applies one piece of meaning: a definition, a relationship, a grain.

-- The join that runs and lies: shipping fees --------------------------
   method                                total fees
   ------------------------------------  ----------
   summed at shipment grain (certified)  USD 415
   orders JOIN lines JOIN shipments      USD 535
   Difference USD 120: SO-104 has two lines, so its USD 120 fee is counted twice.
   customer  net value  fees (joined)  orders (joined)
   --------  ---------  -------------  ---------------
   C-101     2,900      240            2
   C-102     1,650      75             1
   C-103     2,850      220            2
   Net value is right in every row, which is why the query passes a spot check.
